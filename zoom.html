Forum & Salon Topics

Five major educational series covering tax strategies, retirement planning, trusts and asset allocation, college funding, and real estate investment.

First Season: Tax Optimization & Tax-Efficient Strategies

Learn practical tax-saving strategies, tax law fundamentals, and financial planning concepts, including tax preparation and long-term tax efficiency solutions.

Second Season: Retirement Planning & Lifetime Income

Understand retirement concepts, financial products, advantages and limitations, company comparisons, real-world case studies, and strategies for sustainable lifetime cash flow.

Third Season: Trusts & U.S. Asset Allocation

Topics include trusts, domestic and international investments, pre- and post-immigration asset planning, and integrated financial planning strategies.

Fourth Season: College Funding & Education Planning

Focused on college admissions, financial aid planning, scholarship strategies, education funding accounts, and maximizing college financial assistance opportunities.

Fifth Season: Real Estate Development & Investment

Explore real estate taxation, investment structures, market trends, and strategic property investment opportunities across different cities and regions.

The Finova Wealth & Tax Strategy Forum and Wealth Strategy Salon complement each other while offering different formats: the Forum focuses on structured PPT presentations, while the Salon emphasizes interactive discussion and idea sharing.
